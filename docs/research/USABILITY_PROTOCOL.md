# Silukman Image Vectorizer - Usability Study Protocol

**Status:** Draft / Planned
**Ethics Note:** This study involves human participants and requires ethical consideration. Ensure informed consent is collected and institutional review board (IRB) approval is obtained if required by your institution.

## 1. Study Objectives
To evaluate the usability of the Silukman Image Vectorizer desktop application across typical raster-to-vector conversion workflows. 

## 2. Participant Profile
- **Target Size:** 8–15 participants.
- **Demographics:** Mix of graphic designers, researchers, and general users with occasional need for vector graphics.
- **Experience Level:** Participants should have basic familiarity with desktop software; prior experience with vector editing tools (e.g., Inkscape, Illustrator) is beneficial but not strictly required.

## 3. Study Design
- **Format:** Moderated, in-person or remote via screen sharing.
- **Duration:** ~30-45 minutes per participant.
- **Data Collection:** Task success rates, completion times, error counts, behavioral observations, System Usability Scale (SUS) scores, and open feedback.

## 4. Consent and Privacy
- **Informed Consent:** Participants must sign a consent form detailing the purpose of the study, data collection methods, and their right to withdraw at any time.
- **Privacy:** Data will be stored locally. Participant IDs will be pseudonymous. No screen recordings or identifiable information will be retained without explicit permission. No image content will be recorded in the telemetry.

## 5. Tasks and Metrics
- **Tasks:** See `USABILITY_SCENARIOS.md` for the detailed scenarios.
- **Task Success:** Rated as complete (1), partial (0.5), or failed (0).
- **Completion Time:** Measured in seconds from task start to task completion.
- **Error Count:** Number of critical errors or slips made during a task.

## 6. Post-Study Evaluation
- **System Usability Scale (SUS):** A standard 10-item questionnaire to measure overall subjective usability.
- **Open Feedback:** Semi-structured interview regarding pain points, missing features, and overall experience.

## 7. Analysis Plan
- Aggregate task success rates, average completion times, and error counts.
- Calculate the mean SUS score across participants.
- Thematic analysis of open-ended feedback to identify UI/UX improvements.
